FAQ

Honest answers to the questions parents actually ask.

Is my child's voice data safe?

Audio is processed to score pronunciation and then deleted within 24 hours. We never sell voice data, never use it to train third-party models, and never share it with schools or advertisers. The weekly parent report is anonymized — we don't send raw audio to your email.

What ages is this actually for?

Designed for kids 5–10 who can already recognize most letters and sound out simple words. If your child is just learning the alphabet, ReadAlong is too early — we'd recommend a phonics app first and ReadAlong as a follow-up.

How is this different from Khan Academy Kids or SpeechBlubs?

Those are great passive content libraries — kids watch and tap. ReadAlong is the opposite: your child is the performer. The AI listens, scores, and reports back to you. The parent report is the product, not the app itself.

Does it work on iPad / tablet / phone?

Yes — iOS, Android, and a web app for laptops. Phone works best for solo reading; tablet or laptop is nicer for the comprehension quiz.

What if my child stutters or has a speech difference?

ReadAlong is built for reading fluency, not speech therapy. We partner with a small set of speech-language pathologists to make the scoring more forgiving, and we never score on cadence in a way that could shame a child. If you have specific concerns, email us — we'll personally configure your child's profile.

What if my child hates it?

Then it's not the right time. Cancel in two clicks, no questions, no email follow-ups. We'd rather you remember us as the app that respected your kid than the one that annoyed you.
